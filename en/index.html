The Manifesto

Un'Italia neutrale, mediatrice di pace, custode del proprio patrimonio.

Read the manifesto Close the manifesto ↓

Italy holds the largest cultural and artistic heritage in the world with 59 sites recognised by Unesco, in addition to a very high presence of intangible heritage such as all the cultural traditions that make our Nation unique on the international scene. This wealth is not the result of chance but the most sincere expression of the creativity, intelligence and joy of living of the Italians.

The cultural riches of Italy are the fruit of the synergy between the natural beauty of the places and the soul of the Italians. One cannot separate the environment, the nature, the light of the places from the spirit of the inhabitants: each influences and shapes the other. Thus Italy, which enjoys a privileged position at the centre of the Mediterranean, has enriched and has in turn been enriched by those who have inhabited and loved it over the millennia.

You cannot drag Italy into a war in which its irreplaceable artistic treasures could be destroyed.

In terms of International Law: «A neutral State does not participate in wars, abstaining from hostilities and treating belligerents impartially». There have already been international leaders, Popes, Nobel laureates, artists and scientists of recognised standing who have supported this vision.

In this historical period a mass mutation is underway that is not reducible to the theatre of the conservative right and the progressive left, or to religious conflicts. The right and the left have nothing to do with it, the Religions have nothing to do with it. The current mutation of collective consciousness has to do with the different evolutionary levels of individuals. A phenomenon is underway that cannot be measured with the immutable yardsticks and values of historical-social tradition.

Neutralism is a transversal value that runs through, more or less effectively, any political and social group that takes inspiration from the values of secular Revolutions as well as from the values of tolerant Religions. Even if today it seems impossible, we can put Italy forward as a neutral Country. Neutrality does not mean having no army, even of high technological level, but offering oneself as mediator in the resolution of conflicts, repudiating war.

It seems an impossible idea. Probably it is true, but in History events considered «impossible» have sometimes occurred, thanks exclusively to the tenacity and enthusiasm of those who pursued them. It is not wise to give up pursuing a goal merely because it is considered «impossible». There are battles that deserve to be fought for their own sake, regardless of the result.

There are battles that deserve to be fought for their own sake, regardless of the result.

Asking that Italy be a neutral Country means stimulating a debate, giving voice to those, and they are many, who have realised that we can do more to build peace. Let us make this project explicit, bring it to the forefront of politics.

The hope of change is built only if a new political subject is affirmed. The first form of social coherence is represented by language. Around language the polis is built and therefore the political subject. The first step must be taken on a national basis, without neglecting, of course, the possibility of federating, later, with other national realities including outside Europe.

A neutral Italy,
an Italy that mediates peace,
an Italy that protects its heritage.

«He who so tyrannises you has but two eyes, two hands, a body, has nothing more than the least man of the immense and infinite number of our cities, except the superiority you give him to destroy you.

From where has he taken so many eyes, with which he spies on you, if you do not give them to him?

How can he have so many hands to strike you, if he does not take them from you?

The feet with which he tramples your cities, where has he taken them from, if not from you?

How can he have such power over you, if not through you yourselves?

How would he dare attack you, if he did not have your complicity?

What could he do to you, were you not the receivers of the thief who plunders you, accomplices of the murderer who kills you, and traitors to yourselves?»

— Étienne de la Boétie, Discourse on Voluntary Servitude, 1552

Centro Studi · Dialogo accademico

Neutralia partecipa ad AI4Peace

Un paese che voglia essere neutrale non può limitarsi a non firmare alleanze militari. Deve costruire un'economia non dipendente da un singolo blocco, un sistema informativo che non sia branca operativa della propaganda di un campo, e — oggi — strumenti cognitivi che non riproducano i bias geopolitici del paese che li ha prodotti.

Negli ultimi anni l'intelligenza artificiale generativa è diventata la prima fonte di informazione consultata da centinaia di milioni di persone, e il sostegno cognitivo dietro a decisioni educative, sanitarie, giuridiche, giornalistiche e militari. È possibile che uno strumento così pervasivo sia esso stesso neutrale? E se non lo è, cosa significa per un paese che voglia esserlo dipendere da strumenti cognitivi prodotti da paesi che neutrali non sono e non intendono essere?

Neutralia ha aderito a AI4Peace, un'iniziativa di dialogo accademico sull'intelligenza artificiale, la pace e la mediazione. Il documento di lavoro — maggio 2026 — analizza la militarizzazione dei principali modelli generativi, i loro bias geopolitici, le alternative regionali possibili e le architetture cooperative immaginabili. È un contributo aperto al dibattito internazionale, non un punto di arrivo.

Neutralia è aperta al dialogo accademico: scrivici se hai contributi, critiche, controproposte, occasioni di confronto in università italiane o straniere.
